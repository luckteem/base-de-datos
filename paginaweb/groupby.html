3. GROUP BY y Agregación resumir datos

BASICO

Funciones

COUNT() cuenta, SUM() suma, AVG() promedia, MAX() y MIN() extremos.

SELECT COUNT(*) FROM clientes;
AGRUPA

GROUP BY

Agrupa por el mismo valor para contar por grupo.

SELECT pais, COUNT(*) FROM clientes GROUP BY pais;
FILTRO

HAVING vs WHERE

WHERE filtra filas antes. HAVING filtra grupos después.

SELECT pais, COUNT(*) as total FROM clientes GROUP BY pais HAVING total > 5;
